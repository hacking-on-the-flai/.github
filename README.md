## Why we’re here
- Build weird, wonderful, and maybe-questionably-stable prototypes.
- Turn coffee into features.
- Prove that “it works on my machine” is a valid debugging methodology (temporarily).

## Ground rules (unofficial but emotionally true)
- If your demo needs `--force`, it’s still a demo.
- If it trains a model, add +10 wizard points.
- If it works first try, check for hidden bugs — that’s suspicious.

Have fun, break things responsibly, and may your prompts be sharp and your stack traces short 🚀
